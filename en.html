About Sky Loft

Urban luxury in the heart of São Paulo

Located on the 41st floor of the iconic Mirante do Vale building, Sky Loft São Paulo offers a unique stay with a stunning 360° view of the city. Less than 500 meters from downtown, our apartments combine a prime location with exceptional comfort.

More than a place to stay, it's an urban luxury experience. Every unit features a fully equipped kitchen, keyless digital entry, high-speed Wi-Fi and premium amenities. At 150 meters high, you'll find a calm, exclusive retreat away from the bustle of the streets — a true sanctuary in the São Paulo sky.

Prime Location

On the 41st floor of the Mirante do Vale building

Sky Loft São Paulo sits in the iconic Mirante do Vale building, just below the famous Sampa Sky attraction. Our guests enjoy exclusive access to one of the city's most visited destinations.

Sampa Sky features a glass-floor observation deck with 360° views of the São Paulo skyline. Though a separate attraction, our guests enjoy special discounted rates for this unforgettable experience.
